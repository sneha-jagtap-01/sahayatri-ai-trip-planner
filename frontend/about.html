About Sahayatri 🌿

Your AI-powered travel companion that helps you discover meaningful journeys based on your mood, interests, budget and time.

🌿 What is Sahayatri?

Sahayatri is an AI-powered trip planning platform designed to make travel planning simple, personalized and smarter.

Instead of searching through multiple websites, users can simply select their mood, budget, number of days and interests. Sahayatri then creates a personalized day-wise travel itinerary.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, real-world places, maps, budget planning and travel recommendations into one experience.
